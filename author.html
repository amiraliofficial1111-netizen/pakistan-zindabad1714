Biography

Mark H Steffen is the author of The Constitution of God, a manuscript that connects divine constitutional order, human moral freedom, and the purpose of nations in clear, readable language.

The central argument follows a complete arc: from God’s original constitution and the origin of sin, through lineage and restoration in Christ, to purpose in personal life and national destiny.

Essays from the desk are in the Journal. The editorial reviews assess the chapter architecture. For interviews, speaking engagements, and media requests, please use the Contact page.
