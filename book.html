Scope

Why This Book Exists

The Constitution of God is written for readers who want more than slogans: it connects covenant theology, constitutional thinking, and the question of national destiny without flattening any of the three.

Each chapter builds on the last, from the original constitution of God through sin, lineage, the work of Christ, prophetic figures, creation’s structure, and finally God’s desire for humanity and America.
